Prayer for Our President-Elect

Heavenly Father,

We come before You with hearts full of gratitude, placing our trust in Your mercy and love. You have blessed our nation with a new leader, and we turn to You now, asking for Your Spirit to guide him.

Lord, may Donald John Trump be an instrument of Your will, leading with integrity and guided by Your eternal Word. Fill him with Your wisdom, humility, and integrity. May he seek Your truth in all things and lead with a heart that reflects Your compassion and justice. Grant him/her the courage to foster peace and stand firmly for the values that uphold human dignity, the sanctity of life, the strength of the family, and the freedom to worship and serve You.

Lord Jesus, bless our nation so that we may be a light to the world, drawing all to You as we prepare for Your glorious return. Mother Mary, intercede for our president-elect and surround him/her with wise counselors and steadfast allies who, like You, hold faith close to their hearts. Amen.
